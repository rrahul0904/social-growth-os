# Product Specification

## Positioning

**Connect your business once. Give the system a growth objective. It plans, creates, publishes, measures and continuously improves — with governance at every external action.**

## Main product surfaces

### Command Center
Business-outcome dashboard: attributed revenue, qualified clicks, conversions, content velocity, approvals, campaigns and workflow health.

### Growth Agent
A conversational planning surface. The user gives a business objective rather than manually assembling a prompt. The agent gathers evidence, proposes channel-specific strategy and produces a tool trace.

### Workflows
Durable trigger/step automations. Agent reasoning may create or update workflow definitions, but execution is deterministic.

### Calendar
Cross-channel approval/scheduling view with status, creative type and campaign context.

### Library
Asset registry that preserves origin prompt, campaign, product, channel intent and eventual performance.

### Analytics
Closed-loop attribution from impressions → engagements → qualified clicks → conversions → revenue.

### Integrations
Commerce, social, AI and analytics connections isolated behind adapters.

## Differentiation from basic social schedulers

The product is not optimized for “number of posts scheduled.” It is optimized for a measurable business objective and stores enough context to learn at the brand level:
- which hooks win
- which formats win per channel
- which products/audiences convert
- what frequency produces saturation
- what creative combinations drive downstream revenue

## Initial monetization

- Free: demo / one workspace / limited plans
- Pro: small brand, core social connections, agent + workflows
- Team: multiple seats, approvals, higher automation limits
- Agency: multi-client workspaces, RBAC, white-label reporting
- Enterprise: SSO, audit export, custom data retention, private networking
